## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** About a third is authored for this product. The palette generator from a logo, the preview that looks like a real website (nav, hero, card, footer, devices) and the WCAG audit with one-click fixes all match PRODUCT.md exactly. The rest is a 1:1 copy of shadcn CSS variables: 22 tokens × 2 modes = 44 text fields, about 15 selects with CSS vocabulary (Glassmorphism, `[data-img] / .themed-image`), and Tailwind export and JSON import in the editor's path. The result reads as "tweakcn inside Payload", not a tool for a non-technical tenant. The preview, which is supposed to be the centerpiece, is only sticky inside the first field and scrolls away as soon as the user edits what it previews.

**Deterministic scan:** 3 findings across 8.6k lines. 2 true positives: `transition: padding` (AppearancePreviewField.tsx:502) and `transition: max-width` (:516). 1 false positive: "Inter" at TypographyInfoField.tsx:60 is only inside a code sample. The mechanical checks added things the design review didn't name explicitly: 11× `transition: all`, zero `prefers-reduced-motion` in admin fields, status badges with hard-coded light Tailwind colors (#dcfce7/#166534…) instead of `--theme-success/warning/error` (so they break in dark mode), 27 font sizes under 12px (including 8px and 9px), and focus rings at 10% alpha.

**Visual overlays:** not available; the browser step was skipped.

## Overall Impression

The foundation is technically rich and the three signature features are genuinely good. But the screen is built for the author, not for the tenant. The biggest opportunity is to turn 10 equal-weight sections into "choose a look → adjust 5 things → see the result all the time", while keeping the token grid for developers.

## What's Working

1. **The WCAG audit repairs, not just reports.** "Fix" writes the nearest passing color and marks the form dirty (AccessibilityAuditField.tsx:312-321). It turns the "can't publish an unreadable palette" principle into code.
2. **The effects preview renders a website, not a swatch board.** Nav, card, button, footer, device widths and scoped hover (AppearancePreviewField.tsx:347-368). "Show, don't describe" in its purest form.
3. **Style presets are visual cards** with a thumbnail, font sample and description (StylePresetField.tsx:276-509). They're based on recognition, not a dropdown of names.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Destructive overwrites with no confirm or undo; the preset systems fight each other**
- What: `applyPreset` (ThemePreviewField.tsx:319-369) and `applyPalette` (PaletteGeneratorField.tsx:37-52) rewrite every color in both modes with one click. The color preset also rewrites visualEffects, which undoes the Style Preset.
- Why it matters: it breaks principle 2 ("the editor must be unable to break the site"). One mis-click can wipe out an hour of tuning, and nothing says it happened.
- Fix: an inline confirm when there are custom changes ("This replaces 38 of your colors. Replace / Keep mine"), a one-step snapshot with an Undo toast, and color presets that touch only colors while style presets touch only style.
- Command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P0] The cs/en i18n contract is broken**
- What: ThemeColorPickerField.tsx:21 resolves `en` first, so all 44 color labels are English in the Czech admin. ThemePresetImportField, TypographyInfoField and FontLoadingGuideField are entirely English. Plus "Color Picker", "Quick Colors", "on white", "Muted Card", "Select a theme", and aria-labels.
- Why it matters: PRODUCT.md makes cs+en mandatory, and the primary users are Czech tenants.
- Fix: route everything through `useThemeTranslations`, reuse the resolver from ThemePreviewField.tsx:528-536, and add a test that catches untranslated literals.
- Command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Keyboard and screen reader fail in the core pickers**
- What: FontPicker items are a `<div onClick>` (index.tsx:328), and the FontPicker `<button>`s at :233/:299/:310/:338 have no `type="button"`, so they submit Payload's form (a functional bug). FontSelectField and ThemeTokenSelectField are fake listboxes/comboboxes with no keys. Toggles (presets, device, mode, style cards) have no `aria-pressed`. Preview buttons are dead tab stops. `outline:none` with a 10% ring, `:focus` instead of `:focus-visible`. Nothing handles `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Why it matters: a WCAG AA violation, which PRODUCT.md requires.
- Fix: real `<button type="button">`, radiogroup/aria-pressed, arrow keys and Escape, font preview on focus too, `:focus-visible` with a Payload token, and `inert` on the preview.
- Command: `/impeccable audit` → `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The preview isn't the centerpiece while editing; the information architecture is flat**
- What: two separate previews that scroll away before Typography, Effects and Components. 10 equal-weight collapsibles, with the densest one ("Color Mode Settings", 44 fields) the only one open. 7 of 8 cognitive-load checks fail.
- Why it matters: it contradicts principles 1 and 5 (preview first, presets before fine-tuning).
- Fix: a two-column layout with one merged sticky preview on the right. 4 blocks: **Start** (preset/generate) · **Colors** (5 brand colors, with the 22 tokens under "All tokens") · **Text** · **Style**. Export, JSON import and custom CSS move to "Advanced / developer".
- Command: `/impeccable layout` → `/impeccable distill`

**[P1] Misleading and broken UI**
- What: color-field contrast chips measure against white/black rather than the paired token (background #fff = "Low"), so they contradict the real audit and make the palette look broken. Imported custom presets supposedly "appear instantly in the selection above" (themeConfigurationField.ts:432), but nothing reads `customThemePresets` (verified). Status badges and TypographyInfoField use hard-coded light colors and are unreadable in the dark admin. The dark preview meta uses a fixed `rgba(15,23,42,0.7)`.
- Why it matters: false alarms train the user to ignore warnings, and an unkept promise erodes trust.
- Fix: remove the per-field chips or measure against the pair; actually load custom presets into the picker; replace colors with `--theme-*` tokens.
- Command: `/impeccable clarify` + `/impeccable harden`

**Alex (developer):** Imported presets never appear. Tailwind export skips gradients, typography and effects, and copy fails silently. No search across 60+ presets. ## Minor Observations

- Color descriptions are 12px in `--theme-elevation-400`, and many hints are 10–11px in elevation-400/500, which probably fails AA.
- The layout transitions `padding`/`max-width` (AppearancePreviewField.tsx:502, 516) and 11× `transition: all`.
- `CATEGORY_ACCENT` (StylePresetField.tsx:120) is hard-coded indigo/slate, including for the selected label's text.
- The palette generator's seed is set only at mount, so it doesn't follow the primary after a preset is applied. Picking a swatch from the logo needs another click on Generate, and nothing says so.
- Mouse-only drag detection in ThemeColorPickerField means touch drags commit continuously.
- "Hero & background" is in PRODUCT.md but has no fields in the config.
- "Styl předlohy" is an odd label; "Styl vzhledu" would be better.
- `imageStyle` is shown in chips, but the preview contains no image.

## Questions to Consider

1. If a tenant needs about 5 decisions (look, brand color, font, roundness, style), why does the default editor see 44 shadcn tokens and a Tailwind export? Should the token grid be a developer-only feature flag?
2. Should saving be interrupted with a summary while the audit shows failures, or is "can't publish an unreadable palette" just a hope?
3. Three kinds of presets (color, style, custom JSON) overwrite each other. Would one "Look" object with "keep my colors / keep my fonts" locks remove most of the undo and conflict problems at once?
